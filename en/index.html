About

In brief

I am an engineering student at ISEP in Paris, on the cybersecurity track, and I graduate in October 2026. I spent my final semester of classes on Erasmus at ISCTE in Lisbon, studying machine learning and computer science, after an international preparatory cycle that included six months in Prague.

During my internships I mostly built tools, and I came to enjoy what comes afterwards: testing, documentation, handover. I want to design systems that last, in software, the cloud or security.

What my internships taught me

  • I write the success criteria before I start developing, not after.
  • A script validated in test is only truly validated once it has run in the real environment.
  • I automate the execution of a task, not the judgement behind it.
  • A tool is only as good as what remains after I leave: someone to take it over, documentation, a maintenance note.
  • The time AI saves me when writing code, I put back into testing.
